Repertoire

Below is a sample of what we play most often. We shape the night around your crowd — and we’re happy to learn up to two special requests for weddings (first dance, parent dance, etc.).

First dance ideas

  • “At Last” — Etta James
  • “Can’t Help Falling in Love” — Elvis Presley
  • “Perfect” — Ed Sheeran
  • “All of Me” — John Legend
  • “Thinking Out Loud” — Ed Sheeran

Want something different? Tell us your song and we’ll confirm feasibility quickly.

How we pace the night

  • Cocktail: lighter grooves, low-volume classics
  • Dinner: elegant pop/soul, vocals forward, no “club” bass
  • Dancing: high-energy hits + singalongs, minimal downtime
