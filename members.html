Meet the band

We’re a tight 6-piece built for weddings: strong vocals, clean transitions, and a stage presence that feels elevated without being cheesy.

Alyssa Park — Lead Vocals

Alyssa’s warm tone and big-range belts are the backbone of our pop and soul sets. She’s also our on-stage cue leader, keeping transitions smooth and energy consistent.

Pop • Soul First-dance vocal prep

Marcus Hill — Guitar & Vocals

Marcus covers everything from clean funk rhythm to big singalong rock. He’s the “glue” voice in our harmonies and builds tasteful solos that never overpower the dance floor.

Funk • Rock Harmony vocals

Nina Alvarez — Keys

Nina’s keys bring modern polish — synth pads, brass stabs, classic piano — plus she handles subtle ceremony underscoring when requested.

Modern pop textures Ceremony support

Devon Lee — Bass

Devon locks with the kick drum and keeps the groove steady at any volume. He’s a master of “dinner mode” that still feels alive.

Pocket • Groove Tasteful dynamics

Jae Kim — Drums

Jae’s drumming is tight, danceable, and never too loud for the room. He’s great at shifting from elegant cocktail grooves to big pop drops.

Dance-friendly volume Pop precision

Renee Brooks — Sax & Percussion

Renee adds that “wow” factor: sax hooks on modern hits, classic R&B lines, and percussion layers that make the groove feel bigger without adding noise.

Sax feature moments R&B sparkle

How we work

  • Planning call: we align on schedule, must-play songs, and “do-not-play.”
  • Sound approach: tuned to the room — clear vocals, balanced low end, controlled highs.
  • Professional logistics: load-in timing, parking, stage plot, and vendor coordination.
